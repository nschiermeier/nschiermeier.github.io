So, what's up? It's been a long time since I've updated this blog, or even this website in general, but I am back now (maybe). I will try to use this blog to explain why I haven't uploaded in so long, what I plan to do / use this blog for now, and what's next for me personally. 

## Where did I go?

I think the easiest explanation for why I stopped uploading blogs is just because I got busy or a bit burned out, it started feeling like a chore, and it didn't achieve what I wanted it to do. The premise of the way I was using this blog was to try and rewrite my school notes in order to help me study / learn. It was supposed to force me to reread them and put them in a way that was easier to understand. I wanted this to allow me to learn better since I would have to go over my notes to publish them here. However, I don't think this worked as well as I had hoped, if at all.

Additionally, on top of all the homework that I already had to do, this felt like another assignment for each class, so it got to be a bit overwhelming and led to me feeling burned out. Another thing is writing / typing in LaTeX isn't the most fun thing in the world, especially when there are a lot of formulas to copy over. That's maybe the biggest factor that led to it feeling like a chore, typing in LaTeX takes a long time and is kind of clunky and slow, at least for me. One of my classes spring quarter involved a lot of linear algebra, and having to type out matrices for 10 weeks sounds awful.

## What's next for the blog?

This was always a question, since I only planned on uploading the notes for my first year of graduate school. It was supposed to encapsulate that experience, and showcase what UC Irvine teaches in its first year statistics graduate program. I think the way I would like to use this blog from here on out is to just post stuff I find interesting. No expectations, timelines, etc., I will only post content that I _want_ to and _when_ I want to. I hope that this will eliminate the aforementioned problems, since it won't feel like an assignment, and should be stuff I'm more interested in rather than rehashing notes I've already taken (no offense UCI -- This could be stuff in class I find interesting too!). 

I have also thought about using this to post short stories, as that is something I have considered using this space for too. Another original idea I had was to document my progress on my personal projects and keep it as a "log" of my development processes. It could also be quarterly updates on my research, opinions on things I like, etc. An idea to do this effectively might be to implement tags for the posts and allow filtering, so I can actually write whatever I want on here but not have it be unmanageable to readers.
